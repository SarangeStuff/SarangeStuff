SECTION A [50 marks]

Question 1 [12 marks]

A radar station at O tracks a drone. At the instant shown, r = 600 m and θ = 40°. The drone is flying horizontally to the right at 30 m/s, and its speed is increasing at 2 m/s². Its path curves downwards with a radius of curvature ρ = 500 m.

  1. Determine $\dot r$ and $\dot\theta$. Include a diagram showing $\hat e_r$, $\hat e_\theta$ and all the angles you use. (5)
  2. Determine $\ddot r$ and $\ddot\theta$. (7)

Question 2 [10 marks]

Collar A slides along a horizontal bar that is 2 m below the small pulley P. A rope runs from A, over P, and hangs straight down to block B. A is moved to the right at a constant 1.5 m/s.

  1. Define suitable position variables and write the rope-length equation. Include a diagram. (3)
  2. Determine the velocity of B when x = 1.5 m. (3)
  3. Determine the acceleration of B at the same instant. (4)

Question 3 [8 marks]

Crank OA (200 mm) rotates counter-clockwise at a constant 6 rad/s. Rod AB (500 mm) connects it to slider B, which moves in a vertical guide 450 mm to the right of O. At the instant shown, OA is at 30° above the horizontal.

Using the method of instantaneous centres, determine the angular velocity of AB and the velocity of B.

Question 4 [20 marks]

In the four-bar mechanism shown, OA = 150 mm, AB = 350 mm, CB = 250 mm and the fixed pivots are OC = 450 mm apart. At the instant shown, OA is at 60° above the horizontal and has an angular velocity of 10 rad/s counter-clockwise, increasing at 40 rad/s².

  1. Calculate the angles that AB and CB make with the horizontal. (2)
  2. Using a scaled velocity diagram, determine the angular velocities of AB and CB. (7)
  3. Using a scaled acceleration diagram, determine the angular accelerations of AB and CB. (11)

Recommended scales: velocity 1 cm = 0.25 m/s; acceleration 1 cm = 2.5 m/s².

SECTION B [50 marks]

Question 5 [12 marks]

Two smooth pucks slide on a horizontal table. Puck A (0.5 kg) moves in the +x direction at 4 m/s, and puck B (0.8 kg) moves in the +y direction at 2 m/s. At the instant of impact, the line joining their centres makes 30° with the x-axis, as shown. The coefficient of restitution is 0.6.

Determine the speed and direction (angle from the +x axis) of each puck just after impact, and the kinetic energy lost.

Question 6 [12 marks]

A 4 kg block is released from rest on a 30° incline, 1.2 m (measured along the incline) above the free end of a spring of stiffness k = 1500 N/m that lies along the incline. The coefficients of friction between the block and the incline are μs = 0.30 and μk = 0.25.

  1. Determine the speed of the block just before it touches the spring. (4)
  2. Determine the maximum compression of the spring. (6)
  3. Will the block stay at rest at maximum compression, or slide back up the incline? Justify your answer. (2)

Question 7 [14 marks]

A uniform slender bar AB (6 kg, 2 m) has a uniform solid disc (4 kg, radius 0.2 m) welded to it, with the disc's centre at B. The assembly is pinned at A and held horizontal by a vertical cable at B.

  1. Locate the mass centre G of the assembly. (2)
  2. Calculate the mass moment of inertia of the assembly about A. Quote the formulas you use. (5)
  3. The cable is cut. Determine the angular acceleration of the assembly immediately afterwards. (3)
  4. Determine the force exerted by the pin at A at that instant. (4)

Question 8 [12 marks]

A 30 kg uniform solid cylinder of radius 250 mm rests on a horizontal floor (μs = 0.20, μk = 0.15). A horizontal force P is applied at its centre.

  1. For P = 120 N, draw the free-body and kinetic diagrams, and determine the acceleration of the centre, the angular acceleration and the friction force. Show whether the cylinder slips. (8)
  2. Repeat for P = 200 N. (4)
